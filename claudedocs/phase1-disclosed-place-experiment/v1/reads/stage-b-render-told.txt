Corin is at the Toll House.
The flood stands eleven steps below the Toll House door, and Corin writes it in the book.

Brida is at the Rain Market.

Brida weighs out grain that has taken water while Yrsa buys forty feet of rope.

Brida is at the Weighhouse.
Corin is at the Weighhouse.
The salt in the weighhouse store has gone to brine, and Brida seals the door on it.

The Old Cistern: wet at nine feet
p-cistern -> p-market
Yrsa is at the Old Cistern.
Yrsa's line comes up wet at nine feet in a cistern that should be dry to thirty.

Corin is on the Ridge Road.

Corin is at the Shrine of Still Water.

Hallam refuses to take the bell down from the shrine, flood or no flood.

Yrsa is at the Net Loft.
Yrsa takes a diving weight and a lamp from the net loft and signs the peg-tally for both.

The Boat Shed: before light
p-boat-shed -> p-chapel, p-net-loft
Orla is at the Boat Shed.
Yrsa is at the Boat Shed.
Orla's skiff lies out on its mooring, and Yrsa asks nothing of her and goes before light.

The Ridge Road: a bell that does not ring
p-ridge-road -> p-shrine, q-drowned, q-crown
Corin is on the Ridge Road.
No bell rings over the hill on the second morning, and no one has seen Yrsa since the boat shed.

The Drowned Chapel: a candle burned into its tin
p-chapel -> p-boat-shed, p-net-loft
Corin is at the Drowned Chapel.
In the Drowned Chapel the water stands at the sill and a candle has burned down into its tin.

Corin is on the Water Stair.
Orla is on the Water Stair.

The Skiff Mooring: dry inside
p-mooring -> p-water-stair
Orla is at the Skiff Mooring.
The skiff is still tied at the mooring and dry inside, so nothing went out on the water.

The Net Loft: the peg-tally
p-net-loft -> p-boat-shed, p-chapel
Corin is at the Net Loft.
The weight and the lamp are gone from their pegs and the tally is signed in Yrsa's hand.

Dark: the rope cut to nine feet
Yrsa wedges the lamp into a crack and cuts her rope back to nine feet, with the noise of the town somewhere over her head.

The Water Stair: the skiff never left
p-water-stair -> p-mooring, q-drowned
Orla is on the Water Stair.
Yrsa is alive.
Orla climbs the Water Stair saying plainly that the skiff never left its mooring.

Dark: twenty feet down, the water moves
Yrsa is twenty feet down where the water is cold and moving, and moving water has somewhere to go.

Hallam rings the bell for the dead and Corin will not sign the register under it.

Corin is at the Rain Market.

The cistern lid lifts in the market yard and Yrsa comes up out of it after four days under the town's feet.

The Old Cistern: where the flood comes in

Corin is at the Old Cistern.
Yrsa is at the Old Cistern.
Yrsa shows Corin the throat in the cistern wall where the flood comes into the hill, and he enters it in the toll book.
